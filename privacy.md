Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how That Wasn’t on My Bingo Card (“the App”) handles information when you use the App.

Information You Create

That Wasn’t on My Bingo Card does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

Bingo cards and other content you create and save within the App are stored locally on your device. This content is not uploaded to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

If you choose to print or share content using features provided by your device, that activity may be handled by Apple or other apps and services you choose to use.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect certain information in connection with advertising, such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic or performance information.

Google’s collection and use of information is governed by Google’s own privacy policies and applicable consent and privacy settings.

Users may make a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertising from the App.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment card or other payment information.

Apple’s handling of information associated with App Store purchases is governed by Apple’s privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not require users to provide personal information directly to the developer and does not provide user accounts, social networking, or direct communication features.

The App uses a third-party advertising service as described above. Parents and guardians should review the privacy practices and device settings applicable to their children’s use of apps and advertising services.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on certain services provided by third parties, including Google AdMob for advertising and Apple for App Store distribution and in-app purchases. These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Bingo cards and other content saved within the App remain locally on your device unless you delete them, delete the App, or otherwise remove the data through features provided by your device.

The developer does not maintain a server-side copy of your saved Bingo cards.

Third-party services may retain information they collect according to their own policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be posted at the same location with a revised effective date.
